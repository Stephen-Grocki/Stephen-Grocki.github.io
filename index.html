Stephen's Journey at Comanche Trace Country Club

The story of my journey from member to employee at Comanche Trace Country Club in Kerrville, Texas.

About My Member Experience

Upon moving to Comanche Trace in late 2006, my family became golf members of Comanche Trace. At a young age, I was able to learn the foundation of the game and become familiar with the community of golfers in our neighborhood.

Growing up in this community, I was able to meet many people and make friends that I still have today. I was also able to learn the game of golf and develop a passion for it

About My Employment at Comanche Trace

I had worked seasonally at Comanche Trace throughout high school and college. With the asynchronous learning opportunity at Abilene Christian University, I was able to work the final 18 months of my program while working full time at Comanche Trace.

My Job Roles Throughout the Years

Outside Service:

Assist the members with their golf experience by providing them with golf carts, cleaning their clubs, and assisting them with any other needs they may have.

Junior Assistant Pro:

Assist the Head Golf Professional with the day-to-day operations of the golf course. This includes assisting with tournaments, running the golf shop, and assisting with member events.

Computer Systems/Security Analyst:

Configure and maintain the computer systems and security systems for the golf course. This includes maintaining the point of sale systems, improving network security configurations, and assisting with any other technology needs for the golf course.
